## 7. Business and close: 2:23–2:53

**Screen:** Return to the closeout desk. Optional short overlay: **Proposed pilot: $99/month + actual call usage · hypothesis**. End with OffHire and Shivam Gupta.

Existing portals remain useful when they already resolve the request. OffHire handles the conversation still missing. The job is finished. Is the rental?
